Privacy Policy

App: Pallton X80 Drone Guide (com.mohadev.palltonx80droneguide)
Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pallton X80 Drone Guide Android app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. We respect your privacy and keep the App simple: it has no accounts, no sign-up, and we do not collect your personal information ourselves.

Unofficial app notice: The App is an independent, unofficial guide. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the manufacturer of the drone. Pallton and X80 are trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to describe the product the guide covers.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information on our own servers. The App does not require you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

All guide content, checklists, and the PDF manual are stored inside the App. Your checklist progress and settings stay on your device.

2. Permissions

The App uses only these permissions:

  • INTERNET: to display ads and to open web pages you choose to visit.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to check whether your device is online, so the App can tell you when ads or web pages are unavailable.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain data to show and measure ads, such as:

  • Advertising ID and similar device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language)
  • App interaction and ad performance data

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies, not by us. You can learn more here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps

4. Consent and your choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and some other regions), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time using the Privacy options button on the App's home screen, when it is available for your region.

You can also:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Opt out of personalized ads in your device's ad settings.

5. Web pages and external links

Some items in the App may open web pages (for example, search results or government aviation websites) inside the App or in your browser. Those websites are operated by third parties and have their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for their content or policies.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown in the App are intended for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data security and retention

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not store any on our servers. Data handled by third-party services such as Google AdMob is protected and retained according to their own policies.

8. Safety disclaimer

The App provides general information only. Specifications and values shown are for general reference. Always follow the official manual from the manufacturer and the drone laws and regulations in your country.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Please review this page occasionally. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
